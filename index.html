Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding application and forum participation.

What is the core mission of IATSS Forum? ▼

Founded in 1985 in Suzuka, Japan, the IATSS Forum brings together young professionals from ASEAN countries to "Think and Learn Together", promoting mutual understanding, leadership development, and sustainable community growth.

How do Thai applicants apply for the program? ▼

Thai candidates can submit their applications through the Department of Climate Change and Environment (DCCE), Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment, which acts as the official Thai Secretariat.
